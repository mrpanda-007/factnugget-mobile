# FactNuggets User Flows

Version: 1.0

Status: MVP Product Specification

---

# 1. User Flow Philosophy

FactNuggets is designed around two users with different motivations:

## Child

Primary user.

Needs:

- curiosity
- discovery
- fun
- independence
- achievement

The child should feel:

> "I discovered something amazing."

---

## Parent

Economic buyer.

Needs:

- trust
- safety
- confidence
- value

The parent should feel:

> "My child is spending screen time well."

---

# 2. Core Product Loop

Discover
↓
Explore
↓
Complete
↓
Collect
↓
Unlock
↓
Discover More

FactNuggets avoids:

- infinite feeds
- addictive mechanics
- random recommendations
- pressure-based engagement

---

# 3. Child First-Time Experience

## Goal

Allow immediate discovery without requiring parent setup.

---

## Flow

Install App
↓
Open App
↓
Welcome Experience
↓
Choose Explorer Profile
↓
Choose First Discovery
↓
Complete First Pack
↓
Receive Reward
↓
Continue Exploring

---

# Step 1: App Opening

Child opens FactNuggets.

Screen:

Welcome Explorer 🌎
Discover amazing things about our world.
[Start Exploring]

No:

- login
- email
- password

Reason:

Children should experience value before asking parents for commitment.

---

# Step 2: Explorer Identity

The child chooses an explorer identity.

Examples:

- Animal Explorer
- Space Explorer
- Ocean Explorer
- World Explorer

Purpose:

Create emotional connection.

The child is not:

"using an education app"

The child is:

"becoming an explorer."

---

# Step 3: Choose First Discovery

The child sees curated choices.

Example:

What should we discover today?
🐘 Amazing Animals
🚀 Space Adventures
🌊 Ocean Secrets
🦖 Dinosaurs

Important:

This is not an endless category list.

The app highlights a small number of discoveries.

---

# Step 4: Enter Learning Pack

Example:

Ocean Secrets

Screen:

🌊 Ocean Secrets
Discover:
🐙 Octopus
🐋 Whales
🦈 Sharks
🐬 Dolphins
Complete all discoveries
to become an Ocean Explorer.

---

# Step 5: Card Learning Experience

Example card:

🐙 Octopus
Did you know?
An octopus has three hearts.
Two hearts pump blood to its gills.
One heart pumps blood to its body.
Swipe to discover more.

Interaction:

Swipe upward to go to next card

---

# Step 6: Pack Completion

After completing a pack:

Animation:

Discovery Found!
you are now a ocean explorer

---

# Step 7: Pack Completion

When all cards are completed:

Congratulations Explorer!
You completed:
🌊 Ocean Secrets
Reward:
🏆 Ocean Explorer Badge
New discovery unlocked:
🐋 Deep Sea Creatures

---

# Step 8: After Completion

Child receives choices:

Great discovery!
What next?
🚀 Explore Space
🦖 Discover Dinosaurs
🌎 Continue Exploring

No:

- autoplay
- endless cards
- forced continuation

The child chooses.

# 5. Parent Purchase Flow

## Philosophy

Parents should not be interrupted during the child's experience.

The child discovers value first.

Purchase happens naturally.

---

# Trigger Events

Parent conversion happens when:

## Trigger 1

Child completes free content.

Example:

"Can I discover more animals?"

---

## Trigger 2

Child reaches locked content.

Example:

New Discovery Available
🚀 Space Explorer Pack
Ask your parent to unlock.

---

# Purchase Journey

Child Requests More Content
↓
Parent Opens Purchase Screen
↓
Parent Reviews Pack
↓
Parent Purchases
↓
Child Receives New Discovery

---

# Step 1: Parent Gate

Because purchases involve money.

Example:

Parent Area
Answer:
What is 7 + 8?

Purpose:

Prevent accidental child purchases.

---

# Step 2: Parent Preview

Parent sees:

Space Explorer Pack 🚀
Includes:
25 discoveries
Topics:
⭐ Planets
⭐ Stars
⭐ Galaxies
⭐ Space Missions
Age:
5-8 years
Reviewed for children

---

# Step 3: Purchase

Unlock Space Explorer
$X.XX
[Purchase]

---

# Step 4: Confirmation

Parent sees:

Space Explorer unlocked.
Your child can now discover:
🚀 Planets
⭐ Stars
🌙 The Moon

---

# Step 5: Child Notification

Next child session:

Explorer!

🚀 Space Explorer
Ready to explore?

---

---

# 9. UX Principles

## Principle 1

Never make children feel like they are studying.

---

## Principle 2

Never create anxiety around completion.

Avoid:

"You missed your streak."

---

## Principle 3

Every interaction should answer:

"What did I discover?"

---

## Principle 4

Parents should feel:

"I can trust this."

Not:

"I need to monitor this."

---
