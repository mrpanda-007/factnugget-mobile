# FactNuggets Monetisation Strategy

Version: 1.0

Status: MVP Strategy

---

# 1. Monetisation Philosophy

FactNuggets follows a trust-first monetisation approach.

The product must prove value before asking parents to pay.

The core belief:

> Parents do not pay for more screen time. They pay for confidence, quality, and meaningful experiences for their children.

FactNuggets monetisation should:

- preserve child safety
- avoid addictive purchasing behaviour
- maintain parent trust
- reward high-quality content
- create sustainable revenue

---

# 2. Target Buyer

## Economic Buyer

Parents.

Primary characteristics:

- Age: 30–45
- Digitally comfortable
- Concerned about children's screen habits
- Interested in child development
- Willing to pay for trusted educational experiences

---

# 3. MVP Monetisation Model

## Freemium + Premium Learning Packs

The app is free to download.

Users experience the product before paying.

---

## Free Tier

Purpose:

Build trust and demonstrate value.

Includes:

- guest access
- limited learning packs
- local progress
- basic rewards
- exploration experience

Example:

Free Packs:

- Animal Starter Pack
- Weird Facts Starter Pack

---

## Paid Tier

Parents unlock additional discovery experiences.

Paid content is packaged as themed Learning Packs.

Examples:

🚀 Space Explorer Pack

Includes:

- planets
- stars
- galaxies
- astronauts

---

🌊 Ocean Explorer Pack

Includes:

- sharks
- whales
- deep sea creatures
- ocean ecosystems

---

🦖 Dinosaur Explorer Pack

Includes:

- dinosaur species
- fossils
- prehistoric life

---

# 5. Why Start With Pack Purchases?

Advantages:

## Lower Purchase Resistance

Parents understand:

"I am buying a learning experience."

---

## Builds Trust

Parents can test quality before committing.

---

## Matches Product Philosophy

Children explore topics.

Parents purchase meaningful collections.

---

# 7. Conversion Funnel

## Stage 1: Discovery

Parent finds FactNuggets.

Sources:

- app stores
- SEO
- parenting communities
- recommendations

---

## Stage 2: Free Experience

Child:

- completes first pack
- earns first badge
- enjoys discovery

---

## Stage 3: Parent Trust

Parent sees:

- child engagement
- quality content
- safe environment

---

## Stage 4: Purchase Trigger

Child reaches:

- locked discovery
- completed free collection
- interest in new topic

---

## Stage 5: Conversion

Parent purchases:

- individual pack
- collection bundle

---

# 8. Purchase Philosophy

FactNuggets purchases happen through parents.

Children should never feel:

"I need to convince my parent to buy this."

The messaging should focus on:

- discovery
- learning
- exploration

Not:

- missing rewards
- locked achievements
- artificial limitations

---

# 9. Monetisation Do's

FactNuggets should:

✅ Offer meaningful free content

✅ Show quality before asking for payment

✅ Explain exactly what parents receive

✅ Sell collections, not restrictions

✅ Keep purchasing simple

---

# 10. Monetisation Don'ts

FactNuggets should never:

❌ Show advertisements

❌ Sell children's data

❌ Use manipulative child psychology

❌ Create fear around missing content

❌ Lock previously earned progress

❌ Encourage excessive screen time

# 13. Monetisation Risks

## Risk 1:

Parents see it as "just facts."

Solution:

Build strong collections and discovery journeys.

---

## Risk 2:

Children enjoy free content but parents never buy.

Solution:

Create premium packs with deeper exploration.

---

## Risk 3:

Too much paid content hurts trust.

Solution:

Always maintain meaningful free value.

---

# 14. Long-Term Monetisation Vision

FactNuggets should become:

> The trusted digital discovery library parents invest in throughout childhood.

Revenue should grow because:

- children love discovering
- parents trust quality
- families want more worlds to explore

Not because:

- children are pressured to stay longer.
